🧠 flybrain-robot-bridge - Simulated Brain, Real Motion

A brain-inspired camera-to-robot bridge that mimics fruit fly neurons to drive real motor commands via UDP. Perfect for experimenting with biomimetic robotics on Windows.

⬇️ Download for Windows

🤔 What is this?

flybrain-robot-bridge is an experimental controller that copies the neural shortcuts of a fruit fly (Drosophila) to translate camera images and sensory data into motor commands. It's not just your average remote control — it uses a mock neural backend (simulated brain), optical flow (vision), IMU (balance) and UDP (motor output).

Whether you're a hobbyist, a student, or just curious about how biological vision can pilot a robot, this project gives you a glimpse of the future. No programming skills needed to run it — just download and follow the steps.

✨ Features

  • 📷 Optical Flow Vision — detects motion based on camera input, exactly like a fly's compound eyes.
  • 🧪 Mock Neural Backend — a lightweight brain simulation that processes visual data and IMU readings.
  • ⚖️ IMU Feedback — balances and reacts to movement with onboard inertia measurement.
  • 🎮 UDP Motor Control — sends signals to your ESP32 or any network-enabled motor driver.
  • 🔬 MaleCNS Integration (planned) — future release will emulate the male central nervous system for advanced behaviors.

🖥️ How to Run (Windows)

💡 Note: This guide assumes you have an active internet connection and a camera (built-in or USB) to enjoy the full experience.
  1. Find the downloaded file — check your "Downloads" folder. The filename will begin with flybrain-bridge.
  2. Double-click to run — That's it. No installation wizard, no config files. The program will open a small control window.
  3. Allow camera access — When asked, click "Allow" to let the app use your webcam or external camera.
  4. Connect your robot — Make sure your ESP32 or robot is on the same Wi-Fi network. The app automatically broadcasts output via UDP.
  5. Watch it move — As objects move in front of the camera, your robot will respond in mimicry. The mock brain drives the behavior.

🎛️ Controls & Indicators

Once the app is running, a simple dashboard appears with live data:

  • 🧠 Neuron Activity — a small graph shows the simulated neural firing rates.
  • 📊 Optical Flow — visualizes motion direction and speed from the camera feed.
  • 🛰️ IMU Data — if you're using an external IMU, you'll see pitch/roll angles.
  • 📡 UDP Packets — counts of sent motor commands per second.

🛠️ Need help? Troubleshooting

⚙️ Camera not working — Make sure no other app (like Zoom) is using the camera. Close and reopen the bridge.

⚠️ Motor not responding — Confirm your robot's IP address and UDP port matches the defaults (usually 192.168.1.120:8888). Change if needed in the settings.ini file.

🐌 Slow reaction — The bridge uses a small neural net. For faster response, lower the resolution in the settings.
